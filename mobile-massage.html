Mobile Massage Scotland

Massage that travels to you.

Mobile massage is ideal if you would rather stay at home, you are travelling or staying away, you are short on time, or you simply prefer not to travel after a treatment.

I arrive with the treatment table and everything I need. All I ask for is enough space to set up safely and a little time before and after the treatment for setup and packing away.

Your session is still fully tailored around you. Mobile appointments can include deep tissue massage, sports massage, relaxation massage, stretching and mobility work, or a mix of techniques depending on what you need on the day.
