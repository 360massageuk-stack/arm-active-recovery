How it works

Simple, private and easy.

Mobile massage is ideal if you would rather stay at home, you are visiting Edinburgh, you are short on time, or you simply prefer not to travel after a treatment.

I arrive with the treatment table and everything I need. All I ask for is enough space to set up safely and a little time before and after the treatment for setup and packing away.

The treatment itself is still tailored around you — whether you want deep tissue work, sports massage, relaxation, stretching and mobility work, or a mix of techniques.

Not sure if you are too far away?

Try me. Send me your postcode and I will tell you straight away. If I can reasonably travel to you, I will. If there is any extra travel charge, I will always tell you before you book.
