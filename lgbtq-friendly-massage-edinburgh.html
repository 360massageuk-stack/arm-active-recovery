Your Comfort Comes First

Before and during your session

Before treatment, we have a brief conversation about pressure, areas to avoid, injuries and what you would like from the session. You can ask questions, request adjustments or change your mind at any point.

Draping is used professionally and only the area being treated is uncovered. You choose what you feel comfortable wearing or removing. A massage should never make you feel exposed, rushed or uncertain.

For transgender or gender-diverse clients, please feel free to mention any areas that feel sensitive, dysphoria-related concerns, recent surgery or positioning preferences that may affect your comfort. There is never any pressure to explain more than is needed for the treatment.
